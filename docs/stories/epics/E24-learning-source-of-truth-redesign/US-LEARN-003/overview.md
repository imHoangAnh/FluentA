# US-LEARN-003 Overview

## Goal

Rebuild Practice around per-word recap actions so learners can add individual
words to Review during a page drill, while page-level practiced badges are only
persisted when the learner finishes the whole page.

## Scope

- Per-word `Add to Review` during Practice recap.
- Disabled recap states for `Already in Review` and `Added`.
- Page-level practiced marker for the Practice list.
- No end-of-session `Add to Review` CTA.

## Out Of Scope

- Same-day review resume modal.
- Review queue overflow and Level 5 management.
- Full inactive re-add cleanup beyond the durable contract started here.
