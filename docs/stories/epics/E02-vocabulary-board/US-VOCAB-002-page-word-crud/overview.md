# Overview

## Current Behavior

Logged-in users can manage vocabulary boards and pages, but pages cannot store
or display vocabulary words.

## Target Behavior

Logged-in users can select one of their pages and create, list, edit, and
soft-delete vocabulary words through an inline table.

## Affected Users

- Learners maintaining vocabulary lists by unit, chapter, or topic.

## Affected Product Docs

- `docs/product/vocabulary-board.md`

## Non-Goals

- Flashcard card generation or synchronization.
- SignalR notifications.
- Custom columns and optional-column visibility settings.
- Complete spreadsheet keyboard navigation and blur autosave.
